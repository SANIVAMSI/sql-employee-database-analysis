# DAY 03 – SQL BUSINESS ANALYSIS

## 1. Project Overview

The SQL Business Analysis project focuses on analyzing employee and department data using Oracle SQL to generate meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

The project uses the existing EMP_INFO and DEPT_INFO tables to perform advanced SQL analysis, salary comparisons, department-wise performance analysis, employee ranking, and business reporting.

A total of 17 SQL queries were developed and executed to address different business requirements. Each query result was exported into CSV format and validated against expected results.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Analyze employee and department information.
- Calculate department-wise salary statistics.
- Identify employees earning above department and company averages.
- Perform salary ranking and comparison.
- Analyze department salary expenditure.
- Identify salary patterns and employee distributions.
- Generate structured business reports using SQL.

## 5. Business Scenarios Covered

1. Department-wise employee and salary analysis.
2. Highest salary in each department.
3. Top three distinct salary levels by department.
4. Employees earning above their department average.
5. Total company salary expenditure and department contribution.
6. Employees earning above another department's maximum salary.
7. Employees earning above the second-highest salary in their department.
8. Identification of employees with identical salaries.
9. Department-wise employee salary ranking.
10. Department-wise minimum, maximum and salary range.
11. Employees earning above the company average salary.
12. Employee salary classification.
13. Identification of employees without matching departments.
14. Comparison of department average and company average salaries.
15. Department-wise salary statistics.
16. Top two distinct salary levels in each department.
17. Identification of departments with above-average salary expenditure.
